# Brazilian academic CS/IT ecosystems

Starting map of the major Brazilian venues for computer-science and
applied-IT papers. Not exhaustive. Use as a launchpad; always verify
current editions via WebFetch.

## SBC — Sociedade Brasileira de Computação

The umbrella body for Brazilian computing research. Hosts dozens of
symposia, journals, and tracks. Worth surveying for almost any
CS/IT-flavored paper with a Brazilian author or applied focus.

Notable venues (verify current editions):

- **SBSI** — Simpósio Brasileiro de Sistemas de Informação. Applied
  information systems, often industry-academia bridges.
- **SBBD** — Simpósio Brasileiro de Banco de Dados. Data management,
  applied data engineering.
- **SBES** — Simpósio Brasileiro de Engenharia de Software. Software
  engineering practice and research.
- **SBIE** — Simpósio Brasileiro de Informática na Educação. IT in
  education contexts.
- **WCGE / WSCAD / SBSeg / SBRC** — security, networking, distributed
  systems.
- **CTIC** — Congresso Tecnologia em Inteligência Computacional. Applied
  computational intelligence.
- **JAI / CSBC** — broader Brazilian computing congress umbrella.

SBC also publishes journals:

- **JBCS** — Journal of the Brazilian Computer Society. Broad CS scope.
- **RBIE** — Revista Brasileira de Informática na Educação. Education
  + IT.
- **iSys** — Brazilian Journal of Information Systems.

## IEEE LATAM / IEEE Brasil

IEEE has Latin American sections and a Brazilian section. Several IEEE
venues are hosted in Brazil and accept Brazilian-affiliated work.

- **IEEE Latin America Transactions** — broad regional engineering
  journal.
- **CLEI** — Conferencia Latinoamericana de Informática. Latin American
  CS conference.
- **SCCC** — Sociedad Chilena de Ciencia de la Computación / regional
  Latin venues that accept Brazilian work.

## Operations research and applied analytics

- **SBPO** — Simpósio Brasileiro de Pesquisa Operacional. Optimization,
  operations research, applied analytics. Strong for papers about
  decision-support or resource allocation.
- **Pesquisa Operacional (journal)** — SBPO's affiliated journal.

## Embrapii and industry-academia bridges

For papers from companies doing industry-academia bridges (Embrapii's
core mission), look at:

- **Embrapii's own publication channels** — not always traditional
  academic venues, but technical reports, white papers, and case
  collections are sometimes the right home for industry-validated work.
- **PI-ABC** — papers about R&D investment in Brazilian industry.
- **REIT** — Revista de Engenharia, Inovação e Tecnologia. Applied
  technology with industry slant.

## AI-specific venues with Brazilian presence

- **ENIAC** — Encontro Nacional de Inteligência Artificial e
  Computacional. SBC's main AI symposium.
- **BRACIS** — Brazilian Conference on Intelligent Systems. Broader AI
  and applications, English-friendly.
- **STIL** — Symposium in Information and Human Language Technology.
  NLP focus.

## Magazines (for practitioner-flavored work)

When the paper is more practitioner / case-study than original research:

- **Computação Brasil** — SBC's magazine, less formal than the
  journals, accessible to a broader CS audience.
- **InfoQ Brasil** — practitioner-oriented; not academic, but a real
  home for some industry-validated work.

## Cross-cutting notes

- Many SBC venues accept both PT-BR and EN. Some require EN-only for
  the latest editions; always verify on the CFP.
- "Open" vs "rolling" deadlines: journals are usually rolling;
  symposia/tracks are annual with discrete windows.
- Workshop tracks attached to bigger conferences (e.g. WCGE inside
  SBES) are often the best home for applied case studies that wouldn't
  pass a main-track novelty bar.

## Not on this list

This map is intentionally limited to Brazilian-anchored venues. For
papers with strong international fit (and EN content), the search
should also explore IEEE / ACM main conferences in the paper's specific
subfield. That's the agent's call when reading the paper's IS/ISN'T
statement.
